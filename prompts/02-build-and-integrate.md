## Outcome

Produce the smallest working OKF bundle and actual-reader demonstration selected by the contract, with source evidence, negative cases and repeatable checks. Do not replace implementation with a new strategy document. Do not replace the existing Svelte Explorer with the preparation preview.

First validate the contract and source hashes. Inventory the actual instruction stack and preserve unrelated changes. Existing Foundry production rules remain in force for any production claim. The independent rehearsal contract does not override or satisfy them.

## Implementation rules

Keep approved sources immutable. Produce source-native notes and a small justified term set. Author semantic relationships in one source representation; compile JSON-LD and Explorer outputs deterministically rather than maintaining divergent hand-edited copies.

Every directed relationship needs explicit source entity, predicate and target identities; a stable assertion/event identity; labelled scope and authority; a derivation method; precise evidence; and an honest review/freshness state. Local routes are not global identities. Text similarity never creates `owl:sameAs`. Candidate proposals and accepted assertions are different semantic objects or planes; ensure ordinary RDF export preserves that distinction.

Do not conflate planned, completed and cancelled actions. Preserve source missingness and contradictions. Do not manufacture probabilities, human verification, official authority, legally approved uses or absent data.

Use pinned local contexts; reject duplicate keys, executable/custom tags, unsupported aliases/scalars, unmapped semantic terms and unsafe internal routes. Produce explicit errors instead of silent semantic loss. Hash exact bytes for reproducibility; claim JCS or RDF Dataset Canonicalization only when the correct named implementation has actually run.

Use one writer and no more than two read-only workers. Assign precise outputs to workers. A reviewer must examine the raw evidence and frozen candidate, not just the builder's explanation. Use high reasoning only where a failed semantic test or adjudication benefits from it; do not turn every transform into a model call.

## Test in this order

1. Tiny valid fixture and a deliberately invalid counterpart.
2. Actual upstream schema and actual pinned Svelte consumer on the same bytes.
3. Full selected source slice, evidence reconstruction and declared coverage reconciliation.
4. Candidate, action-state, missingness, identity, source-hash, context and projection regression tests.
5. The intended human journey: find, inspect, follow a relationship, check its source, see uncertainty, use keyboard/mobile layout, and restore route/source state.
6. One final affected full suite against a frozen candidate; no repeated full rebuild without changed input or a diagnosed condition.

Use a new profile identity for genuine extensions; do not republish a partial/customised frozen Bundle Wiki mirror under the canonical URI. Run actual consumers rather than substitutes.

## Delivery

A local artefact containing authored notes, generated bundle, input/profile/consumer locks, source locators, test receipts, a brief README, the demo route and known limitations. Record actual commands and model configuration. Keep private inputs and credentials out of public outputs. Ask for publication approval only after the local result is concrete and the publication gates are satisfied.
